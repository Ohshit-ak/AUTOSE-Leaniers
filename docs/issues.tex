\section{Summary}

This report collects every open issue as of commit \code{40b97a4} plus the uncommitted changes of
19 November (Section~\ref{sec:nov19}). The pipeline results come from
the L0 v11 + chain-v5 runs (Qwen~2.5~7B, 18 SRS, seeds 42--44; see \file{results.md}). The harness
defects come from the new property-based and metamorphic test suite. All numbers are sums over the
18 SRS of the per-SRS mean over the three repetitions, in the \emph{strict} setting unless stated.
\file{results.pdf} (13 November) listed Issues 1--7 for chain-v4; Section~\ref{sec:status} shows
which of them are still open.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PO3 is the main failure.} 101.0 of 113.3 L2 component failures (89\,\%) are PO3.
        PO3 is proved for 408.7 of 522 components (78\,\%), the same rate as chain-v4 (P1).
        \emph{New (19 Nov):} the Lean-only triage of the 214 distinct failures finds 80 wrong code,
        77 apparently unsatisfiable specs and only 55 where tactics may be the problem (12 of them proved
        by stronger tactics). Most PO3 failures are not a proof-search problem.
  \item \textbf{The results overstate what the model does.} 215 of 517 specs (42\,\%) are weak. Among
        the components that pass PO3, the harness skeleton alone wrote 281.7 and the model 127. Only
        64 non-trivial specs are verified with code the model wrote (P2, P3).
  \item \textbf{Decomposition is barely exercised.} 20 two-component chains are accepted out of 502
        specs, and 12.7 are verified end to end (P4).
  \item \textbf{L0 v11 regressed on three SRS} (gamma j, ertms, dii). Investigated 19 Nov: 19 specs lost,
        9 because v11 no longer accepts them at L0, 9 at L2 PO3, 1 at L1 PO2 (P5).
  \item \textbf{Code changes for P1, P2 and P10 are written but not run with a model}, and the working
        tree is uncommitted, so a run started now records \code{git\_dirty: true} (N2).
  \item \textbf{28\,\% of L0 entries never reach L1}: 718 entries, 615 compile, 517 pass L0.5 (P6).
  \item \textbf{The test suite found 7 harness defects} (10 xfail tests). Two of them change reported
        numbers (H1, H2) and one drops requirements in the parser (H3).
\end{itemize}

\section{Pipeline and results issues}
%=====================================================================

\issue{P1. PO3 fails on about 1 in 5 components}
PO3 (\code{Contract.Satisfies c f}) is the obligation that the implementation meets its contract.
It is by far the largest L2 failure class, and chain-v5 did not change its rate.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}
\toprule
Run & components & PO3 proved & PO3 failed & other L2 failures \\
\midrule
chain-v4 strict           & 456.7 & 352.0 (77\,\%) &  95.0 &  9.7 \\
v11 + chain-v5 strict     & 522.0 & 408.7 (78\,\%) & 101.0 & 12.3 \\
v11 + chain-v5 fallback   & 536.3 & 417.7 (78\,\%) & 105.0 & 13.6 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The other L2 failures are syntax (4.3), unknown field (4.0), type error (2.7) and unknown name (1.3).

\evidence PO3 failures per SRS (strict): eirene\_fun\_7-2 20.3, peppol 11.0, gamma j 9.7, tcs 8.0,
gemini 7.3, microcare 7.3, video search 7.3, eirene sys 15 6.7, ertms 6.7, phin 6.3, dii 3.0,
cctns 2.3, themas 2.0, qheadache 1.0, keepass 1.0, blitdraft 1.0. In two-component chains the
failing component is almost always the second one, which consumes \code{Mid}: for example
\code{ApplyRoleAndPrivilegesContract} in \code{UC-administrator-add-user}, and
\code{ShowResults…}/\code{FormatResults…} in video search. Some specs also fail PO3 as a single
component. \code{UC-administrator-add-user} looks contradictory. \code{…-remove-user},
\code{3.1.3/S1}, \code{3.1.3/S3}, \code{SI-1.2}, \code{REQ-1}, \code{REQ-4} and \code{REQ-6} are not
yet checked.

\textbf{Status (19 Nov).} The split is done (Section~\ref{sec:nov19}): 80 wrong-code, 77
unsatisfiable-spec, 55 tactics (12 proved by \code{CF\_PO3\_TACTICS=2}), 2 unevaluable.

\fix (a) For the 80 wrong-code pairs, run chain-v5 with \code{CF\_CEX\_FEEDBACK=1} (and
\code{CF\_PO3\_TACTICS=2}), 3 seeds, and compare against the stored runs. (b) For the 77 unsatisfiable
specs, add a realizability check at L0.5, so that L0 rejects a \code{post} that no output can meet
for an input in \code{pre}. ``Unsatisfiable'' is a search result: confirm a sample by hand first.
(c) For the 43 tactics cases with no counterexample, inspect a sample. A larger input search may turn
some into wrong-code.

\issue{P2. Weak specs still count as verified}
215 of 517 specs (42\,\%) are weak: Lean accepts an implementation made only of copied inputs and
constants. 12 of them only pass data through (copy-ok). v11 sends a weak spec back once, then accepts
it. Counting only non-trivial specs, 193.3 of 302 (64\,\%) are verified end to end (strict), and
200.0 (66\,\%) with fallback.

\fix Report the non-trivial rate as the headline number. Add an L0.5 check that rejects or repairs a
spec whose witness is trivial, instead of accepting it after one round of feedback.

\issue{P3. Most verified code is the harness skeleton}
Of the 408.7 components that pass PO3, the spec-directed skeleton alone produced 281.7 and the model
contributed to 127. Of the 193.3 non-trivial specs verified end to end, 64.0 contain code the model
wrote (70.6 with fallback). This split is by design and is reported separately. However, defect H1
credits some skeleton fields to the model, so the model share is slightly too high.

\textbf{Correction (19 Nov).} H1 is fixed, but the ``64.0 $\to$ 63.0 / 70.6 $\to$ 69.6'' correction
written in \file{results.md}, MEMORY.md and above is itself wrong. \code{CI-1} was already stored
as \code{harness\_only}, so the correction subtracted it a second time. Recomputing from the records
(\file{docs/analysis/p4\_p3\_split.md}) gives \textbf{64.0 strict and 70.7 fallback}: 34.3 model-completed
+ 29.7 model-fully, and 129.3 harness-only, out of 193.3.

\fix Replace 63.0 / 69.6 with 64.0 / 70.7 in \file{results.md}.

\issue{P4. Decomposition is rarely exercised}
Of 502 L1-accepted specs, 441 are single (not split), 41 are not decomposable (identity split), and
only 20 are two-component chains. 12.7 chains are verified end to end. 15 specs fail at L1: PO2 10.3,
visibility 2.0, unknown field 1.3, type error 0.7, syntax 0.7. In video search, list properties
(sorted, permutation, every wanted item in the results) do not survive \code{Mid}, so PO2 fails.

\fix Real architecture work belongs to the graph path (P10). For the chain path, require \code{Mid} to
carry every property the second component needs, and check this statically before L2.

\issue{P5. L0 v11 regressions on gamma j, ertms and dii}
End to end, gamma j drops from about 53 to 49.3, ertms from 33 to 28--29, and dii from 5 to 3. The
ablation (chain-v5 on the v10.1 specs) does not show these drops, so they come from L0 v11.

\textbf{Investigated (19 Nov,} \file{docs/analysis/p5\_l0\_regression.md}\textbf{).} Over the three
SRS, end to end drops from 91.0 to 80.7, but non-trivial end to end rises from 39.7 to 40.7. So
v11 loses mostly weak specs. 19 specs are lost: 9 are no longer accepted at L0 (they no longer compile,
or now fail or are flagged at L0.5), 9 fail PO3 at L2 and 1 fails PO2 at L1. 11 of the 19 were weak
under v10.1.

\fix Treat this as an expected side effect of the stricter L0 unless the 9 L0 losses are wrong
rejections. Check those 9 by hand (for example gamma j \code{DD-password}, flagged only because the
data test does not apply to its shape).

\issue{P6. L0 loses 28\,\% of entries, and the rate hides it}
Over the 18 SRS, L0 v11 produced 718 entries. 615 compile, 446 reject the bad example, 517 pass
L0.5, 64 are flagged and 137 fail. The 201 entries that never reach L1 do not appear in the
``end to end'' rate, which is computed over the 517. These L0 compile failures recur in every run:
\code{5.5/R1}, \code{DD-shipping-date}, \code{DD-credit-card-expiry-date}, \code{DD-location} and
\code{UC-customer-checkout-an-order}.

\fix Report the end-to-end rate over parsed requirements as well as over L0 specs. Look at the
recurring compile failures one by one.

\issue{P7. Parser coverage}
peering and get real 0.2 each parse to a single requirement, and that requirement is flagged at L0.5,
so 0 specs reach L1. Under chain-v4, peering's one spec was verified. More generally, the parser keeps
few SRS items: the modal-verb rule drops most of them (Issue~4 in \file{results.pdf}). The parser also
drops common concurrency wording (H3).

\textbf{Confirmed 19 Nov.} Because of this, the PO3 triage and the P8 sample cover only 16 SRS: all
12 chain reps of these two documents have empty \file{l1l2/} folders (``0 of 0 accepted specs''), and
L0 has only \file{rep1} for them. The stored runs are from before the P7 parser change (peering
$+1$), so peering's L0 should be rerun with the current parser.

\fix Fix H3. Rerun L0 on peering with the P7 parser. Report get real 0.2 as out of scope, with the
reason. Do not add a rule specific to one document: the parser must work for any SRS.

\issue{P8. Specs are not checked against the requirement's meaning}
Compiling, rejecting a bad example and passing L0.5 do not show that a spec says what the requirement
says. A verified spec can add conditions the text never states, or leave some out. No checked spec
has been reviewed by hand for meaning.

\textbf{Status (19 Nov).} Grounding: 125 of 517 accepted specs (24\,\%) use constants the requirement
does not contain (hard-coded messages, credentials, thresholds), and 33\,\% of ``strong'' specs do.
So L0.5 ``strong'' partly means ``strong because of invented constants''. Against the gamma j gold
file: 13 equivalent, 4 different, 2 weaker, 1 missing, 5 not accepted. One ``different'' verdict
(\code{DD-quantity}) was made by Claude and needs a human check. The gold file's authorship is not
recorded. Under the open-weight-only rule, the team must confirm it was hand-written before it is
used as a reference.

\fix Two team members fill \code{rater1}/\code{rater2} in \file{docs/analysis/p8\_fidelity.csv}
(60 specs, independently), then run \code{python evaluation/kappa.py}. No model fills these columns.

\issue{P9. Run-to-run variance is understated}
Every L1/L2 repetition reads the same L0 run (\file{qwen-v11/rep1}), so the min--max ranges cover
L1/L2 only. L0 has never been repeated.

\fix Run L0 three times (seeds 42--44) and chain each L1/L2 repetition to its own L0 run.

\issue{P10. The graph path is out of date}
The committed gamma j graph (\file{runs/0000 - gamma j/graph/}) is still graph version 1. L1-v1 (the
graph architect) has not been rerun since graph v2 and degrading were added, and PO2 was proved in
none of its runs. \code{3.5.3/S2} and \code{3.1.3/S1} break the harness's own trace proof with
``Expected type must not contain free variables''; the same error appears in the peppol v10.1 specs.
Its cause is not known. The state entities produced by graph v2 have not been reviewed by hand.

\fix Rebuild the graph (\code{-{}-until graph}), rerun graph L1 three times with Qwen, and find the
cause of the free-variable error.

\issue{P11. Nothing rewards a stronger contract}
A contract equal to the L0 conditions makes all obligations easy. Nothing yet rewards a contract that
says more than the requirement or relates operations to each other. With degrading, PO2 is only as
strong as the contracts that were not degraded, so a run must report how many operations were
degraded.

\textbf{Status (19 Nov).} A model-free payoff is defined and measured
(\file{docs/design/p11\_mechanism.md}). It is 0 if the spec is unsatisfiable, vacuous, ungrounded or
untestable, and the mutant-kill rate otherwise. Mean 0.42 over all 718 L0 entries, 0.78 over the 384
that pass every gate. It is a measurement only: nothing in the pipeline selects or rewards with it yet.
See N1: gamma j's scores look wrong.

\issue{P12. Open decision: default setting}
\code{-{}-fallback-single} is the best setting (400.7 of 517 end to end; 200.0 non-trivial). Whether it
becomes the default is not decided.

\issue{P13. Not built}
Not built yet: the last pipeline step (Lean implementation to Python), \file{harness/reverify.py},
\file{evaluation/metrics.py}, and a benchmark system in \file{benchmark/bank\_ledger/}.
There is also no truly held-out SRS for L0: video search was seen during v9, and the other 16 were not
used for design but have now been run.

\issue{N1. Suspected defect: strength scores on gamma j}
\file{evaluation/strength\_score.py} marks 60 of 74 gamma j entries as vacuous (0 mutants killed),
with a kill rate of 0.02. On every other SRS, 0--3 entries are vacuous and the kill rate is 0.58--0.80.
L0.5's own vacuity check passes 59 of these gamma j specs. The scorer probably fails to build or
compile the mutants for gamma j's spec layout, and counts that as ``not killed''. Until this is
checked, the overall P11 numbers (mean 0.42, 74 vacuous) include this error.

\fix Run the scorer on one gamma j spec (for example \code{1.4/S2}) and look at the mutant Lean output.
Count a mutant that does not compile as untested, not as surviving.

\issue{N2. Uncommitted, unrun harness changes}

started now records \code{git\_dirty: true} and is not reproducible from a commit.

\fix Run the test suite, commit, then run the GPU follow-ups from the commit: chain-v5 with
\code{CF\_CEX\_FEEDBACK=1 CF\_PO3\_TACTICS=2}, \code{CF\_WEAK\_ROUNDS=2}, graph L1 (\code{--with-graph}),
and L0 $\times$3 (P9).

\code{cd harness \&\& python -m pytest} gives \textbf{1437 passed, 0 failed, 10 xfailed}

must be removed.

\begin{small}
\begin{longtable}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.6cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.4cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{7.6cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.0cm}@{}}
\toprule
& Where & Defect & Effect \\
\midrule
\endhead
H1 & \code{skeleton.} \code{\_impl\_record} &
  Looks for \code{ with } in the record body with only brackets masked, so a string literal such as
  \code{"sent with care"} is taken for \code{\{ x with … \}} and every field is credited to the model. &
  \textbf{Wrong model/skeleton split}; 6 stored records. \\
H2 & \code{report()} (run summary) &
  Takes its rows from the first repetition and fills a missing metric with 0. ``Strength of chain
  architectures'' shows 2.50 instead of 5.00 with (chain, none), and disappears with (none, chain). &
  \textbf{Wrong reported numbers}; 10 of 89 recorded runs. \\
H3 & parser, \code{COUNT\_RE} &
  ``100 concurrent users'', ``500 simultaneous users'' and ``1,000 simultaneous requests'' are not
  read as quantities (the regex wants the noun right after the number), so they are dropped as having
  no quantity to formalise. Only ``100 users concurrently'' reaches the \code{concurrency} check. &
  \textbf{Requirements silently dropped}; 3 xfails. \\
H4 & \code{validate.} \code{field\_types} &
  Keeps a trailing \code{-{}- comment} in the type (\code{Nat -{}- in cents}), so the mutant, witness and
  skeleton code no longer see \code{Nat}. \code{req\_graph.structures} already strips the comment. &
  7 of 576 accepted v11 specs. \\
H5 & \code{validate.} \code{literal\_fields} &
  Toggles ``in string'' on an escaped quote, so \code{\{ name := "a\textbackslash",b", qty := 1 \}} is
  split inside the string. \code{one\_line} and \code{\_balanced} handle the escape. &
  Wrong fields for such literals. \\
H6 & \code{validate.} \code{check\_}\allowbreak\code{contradictions} &
  \code{o.total = o.total + 1 - 1}, which holds for every value, is reported as ``can never be true'':
  the second \code{\_SELF\_EQ} pattern has no look-ahead for a following operator. &
  False contradiction; a correct spec is sent back. \\
H7 & \code{apply\_fixups} &
  (a) A field written 3 or more times needs two passes, because one \code{re.sub} removes only every
  other repeat, so the fixups are not idempotent. (b) A field named \code{deriving} is renamed to
  \code{deriving\_}, and \code{\_always\_derive} then rewrites that line to
  \code{deriving \_ : Nat,} \code{DecidableEq, Repr}. &
  Broken Lean after fixups; 2 xfails. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{small}
